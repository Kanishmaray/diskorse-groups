How do I manage the fatigue that never seems to go away, even a year after treatment ended?

Treatment ended fourteen months ago and everyone keeps telling me I should feel back to normal by now. I am not. Some days I cannot get through an afternoon without lying down. I do not know what is reasonable to expect anymore, or what is worth raising with my oncologist versus just accepting.

Quick poll: has anyone actually gotten a straight answer from their oncologist on this?

This is called cancer related fatigue and it is real, not in your head and not a sign you are doing recovery wrong. Ask your oncologist specifically about ruling out thyroid and anemia before assuming it is permanent.
